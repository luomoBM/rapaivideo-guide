How to Turn Two Photos Into a Rap Music Video With AI

Field notes - updated November 2026

Making a rap music video used to mean hiring a videographer, an editor, and someone who can actually write bars. In 2026 the whole thing fits in a browser tab.

The pipeline in 3 steps

  1. Pick two photos — one face per rapper, front-facing, similar distance to camera, no sunglasses.
  2. Pick a stage and a topic — club, street court, or studio; birthday roast, breakup flex, or gym motivation.
  3. Generate — the AI writes original rap lyrics, generates a beat, and renders a lip-synced duo video in about three minutes.

AI Rap Video runs the whole pipeline in the browser: upload two selfies, pick the stage and topic, get the finished rap video. Free credits to start, and you can upload your own song if you already have a track.

Practical tips

  • Keep both faces at similar scale — the lip-sync quality depends on it.
  • Vertical export for TikTok/Reels, horizontal for YouTube.
  • If you upload your own song, keep it under three minutes.
